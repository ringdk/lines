## 2026-11-05 — r1

Lines is in Serafina's apps row: it helps you learn your lines. Add a script
(a PDF, pasted text, or a link to a Google Doc shared with "anyone with the
link"), check it read who says what, and pick your part. Then rehearse: I
read the other parts, you give your line by typing it (or just its first few
words, or the first letter of each word), or by tapping the suggestions. Tap
**Line!** when you're stuck. After each line you see it as written, with
anything you got wrong in bold. The suggestions get harder as you learn a
line, and the meter at the top shows how well you know it. Your scene
partner sits in the chat beside it. Try: "Take it from 'This was my home
too'", "Drill the lines I keep fluffing", "How am I doing?"
